Every change to PixelCake that a person can notice is written down in the changelog of the repository, newest first, as it is made: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/pixelcake-dev/pixelcake/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). It is in English, and it names the controls of the interface by their labels in both languages.

## Versions {#versions}

The app is in an early stage: its changes are listed under one heading, Unreleased, until the first release. The language of the terminal has a version of its own, NCL v0.5, which `help` prints on its first line; what each version of the language added is listed in the [overview of the terminal](../terminal/index.md#version).
