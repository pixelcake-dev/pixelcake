# Code of conduct

PixelCake is built for people who draw networks, teach them, or are learning them. Whoever takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented, is expected to:

- be kind, patient and specific, with beginners above all: a question that looks basic is how everyone starts;
- criticise ideas and code, never the person who wrote them;
- assume good faith, and ask before concluding the worst;
- accept that the maintainer decides what fits the project, and explains why.

Not accepted: insults, harassment, discrimination of any kind, personal attacks, sexual language or images, publishing other people's private information, and deliberately disrupting the work of others.

## Reporting

Write to pixelcake.dev@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. Behaviour that breaks this code can lead to comments being edited or removed, and to a person being blocked from the project.
